## 7. Cookies

Knitwright uses only cookies that it needs to work: sign-in session cookies, a cookie that remembers which brand you are working in, and the cookies the bot check sets during sign-up and sign-in. There are no analytics or advertising cookies, so we do not ask for cookie consent.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. You can delete designs and your whole account yourself, and download any tech pack as PDF, Excel or SVG. For anything else, write to the privacy contact above; we reply within one month.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or, in the EU, to the data protection authority where you live or work. We would like the chance to help first.

## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers. Each brand's data is kept apart by database rules that are tested automatically. Access to the full database is limited to the server. We will tell you and the regulator about a personal data breach where the law requires.

## 10. Changes

We will post changes to this notice here and, for significant changes, tell you by email or in the app.
