# FashionInsta output, 2026-10-14 (benchmark)

What fashionINSTA plus a chat assistant produced for two designs. Knitwright's own output must beat these.

- `pattern_1597.dxf` and `Knitwright_Crew_Tee_EU48_TechPack.pdf`: men's crew tee, EU 48, Kim & Kim knit sloper, 5% stretch reduction. Four pieces: front, back, sleeve, neckband.
- `pattern_1598.dxf` and `Football_Jersey_No10_EU48_TechPack.pdf`: football jersey on the same block, body +3.3 cm, collar at 85% of the neckline. Design: white base, 3 cm red and royal blue polka dots at a 7 cm pitch in alternating rows offset half a step, navy number 10 with a white outline (10 cm front, 25 cm back), name above the back number, navy rib collar, navy sleeve bands, crest and 30 x 7 cm sponsor placeholders.

Known weaknesses: six POMs left as "confirm" (HPS to hem, shoulder length, neck width, front drop, sleeve opening, seam allowance); one size only, no grading; the DXF carries no artwork, so there are no print files; no per-player files; no fabric stretch or shrink data behind the 5% reduction.
