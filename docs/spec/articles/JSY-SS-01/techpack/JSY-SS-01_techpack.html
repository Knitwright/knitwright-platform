Key construction features

  • Shoulder, sleeve and side seams: 4-thread overlock (ISO 514), 12–14 SPI.
  • Crew neck: 1x1 rib, 20 mm finished, CB join, back neck taped and coverstitched.
  • Hems: 2-needle coverstitch (ISO 406); body 25 mm, sleeve 20 mm.
  • Labels: heat-transfer neck label; care label in wearer's left side seam, 80 mm above hem.
  • Panels printed oversize with 10 mm bleed, cut flat after pressing; left and right sleeves cut separately.

QC checklist

Sampling

Class A (from 1 piece): 100% inspection. Runs: AQL 0 critical / 2.5 major / 4.0 minor, general inspection level II (ISO 2859-1). (R-QC-001)

Measure

POM A, B, C, D, E, H, I against page 8 tolerances. Outside tolerance = major defect.

Colour

Each colour role vs the strike-off, ΔE CMC(2:1) ≤ 1.5. Record the reading.

Photos for every order

Front · back · neck label · care label · side-seam close-up · print-edge close-up.
