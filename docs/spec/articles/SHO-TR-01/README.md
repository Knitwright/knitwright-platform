## What is here

- `article.json` — the ONBOARDING.md step 1 brief. Only values with a citation in its `sources` list; everything the
  build pack does not publish is empty. `candidates` holds what the build pack publishes as illustrative, an example
  or a placeholder, cited row by row, kept for the factory conversation and deliberately not part of the article.
- `rulebook.md` — the article's rules, every one `draft`, every one with its source.

## What is not here, and why

- **No POM table.** Nothing published measures this garment, and a guessed measurement reaches a factory as if it were
  one. An empty POM table is the correct state until ONBOARDING.md step 2 brings the factory's size chart.
- **No parametric script.** `lib/parametric` answers "not drafted yet" for this code, with the reason and the rule
  (`R-PAT-101`). The points of measure a drafter would need are listed in `article.json`.
- **No pattern, tech pack or sample.** Steps 3 to 9 of ONBOARDING.md have not been run.
- **Not `live`.** Owner decision 10: no article goes live without a factory-checked master pattern, and none exists.

- The waistband (elastic, drawcord or both), the side split and the pocket list need option codes before they can be offered.
- pom.csv's waist and inseam figures are illustrative. Do the factory's own numbers agree with 380 mm waist and 127 mm inseam at M?
- Size range and sample size: the build pack publishes neither for this article (decision 0066, owner question 1).
- Men's, women's or unisex, and the fit word (regular, athletic): the build pack publishes neither.
- The POM table with grades and tolerances from the factory's own size chart (ONBOARDING.md step 2).
- The construction table, seam allowances and thread the factory really uses.
- Fabric: the build pack says every fabric code, weight and blend is a placeholder until the Phase 0 factory test.
