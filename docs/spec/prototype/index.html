Why? · Sponsor crosses side seam

Front and back are printed and cut as separate panels, then sewn. Sewing shifts panels by up to 3 mm, so artwork across the seam may not line up.

Best practice: keep logos and text 15 mm inside the seam. Stripes can cross if a small offset is fine.
